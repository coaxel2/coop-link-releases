Everything the old link cable did, over the internet

Coop Link runs your games in modified emulators (melonDS, mGBA, Azahar) and connects up to 8 players in one room. Each player keeps their own save and their own story.

👥

See each other

Your friends appear on your map with their name and their walking animation, wherever you both are.

⇄

Trade on the spot

Face a friend, press A, choose Trade. Trade evolutions included.

⚔

Real 1v1 battles

Each player picks their own moves and switches, on both screens. Nothing is lost afterwards.

➜

Join a friend

Hold L and R, pick a friend, and you arrive next to them.

★

Friends list

Create an account and see which friends are online, since when, and what they are playing.

🎲

Make it yours

Randomizer with a shareable game code, rebalancing, patches, two-player Nuzlocke, level cap and more.

Ready in three steps

No account needed to play. The launcher updates itself.

Download

Get the launcher for your system, unzip it and open it.

Add your game

Click the + and choose your own game file. The launcher recognises it.

Play together

One player clicks Host and sends the room code, the others click Join and type it.

Questions

Where do I get the games?

From your own cartridges or game cards. Coop Link includes no game, no BIOS and no key, and links to none.

Do my friends need the same version?

Yes, everyone in a room uses the same launcher version. The launcher offers each update by itself.

Does it change my game files?

No. The emulators read the game memory and draw your friends; your game files stay as they are.

Is it free?

Yes. It is built on the open source emulators melonDS, mGBA and Azahar; their modified sources are attached to each Windows release.
